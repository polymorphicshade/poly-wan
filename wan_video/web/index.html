Models

Each operation needs a model family: VACE does text, image, control, pose and editing; T2V and I2V are the dedicated text- and image-to-video models; Animate is Wan2.2-Animate. Variants download automatically the first time they are used. VACE 1.3B runs on 8 GB GPUs; GGUF Q4 variants bring the 14B models to 12–16 GB cards.
